# 🚀 Number Nebula

**A 2-player space duel where you solve sums and spell words to beat your rival.**

Built at **Vibe Coding with Google AI Studio** (GDG on Campus, Peshawar), then upgraded with combo attacks, power-ups, a boss battle and a Word Builder mode.

---

## 🎮 Game modes

| Mode | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Math Duel** | Solve the sum at the top of your side, then shoot the alien carrying the answer |
| **Word Builder** | Read the clue (a picture for kids), then shoot the letters in order to spell the word |
| **Brain Mix** | Maths and spelling take turns, so you must switch your brain fast |

## ✨ Features

- **Same-screen multiplayer**, or play solo against **Cosmic Bot**
- **Combo attacks:** 3 correct in a row sends Freeze, Fog or Meteor Rush at your rival
- **Power-ups:** Shield, Double Shot, Slow-Mo, Hint
- **5 levels** ending in a **boss battle** (first boss down earns +1000)
- **Per-player difficulty** (Kids 7+, Teen, Genius), so a child and an adult can play a fair match
- **Awards** at the end: Sharpshooter, Speed Demon, Combo King, Saboteur, Boss Slayer
- Works on laptops (keyboard) and phones/tablets (touch buttons)

## ⌨️ Controls

| | Move | Fire |
|---|---|---|
| **Player 1** | `A` `D` | `W` |
| **Player 2** | `←` `→` | `↑` |

`P` pause · `M` mute

## ⚙️ Tech

- **HTML5 Canvas + vanilla JavaScript**, one single file, no frameworks, no build step
- **Web Audio API** for all sound effects and music (no audio files)
- A classic **game loop** running about 60 times a second: update → check collisions → draw

## 🏃 Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. That's it.
